apoio_EN29A: Many online services now ask users to prove their age before they can sign up. The goal is understandable: children should not be exposed to content and features designed for adults. The methods, however, raise new concerns. Uploading an identity document or a selfie to a website creates fresh records that can be leaked or reused.
Privacy advocates argue that age checks should collect as little information as possible. Some systems simply confirm whether a user is above a certain age, without revealing a name or a date of birth. Others rely on estimates made from a photo, which are then deleted.
No method is perfect. A balance must be struck between protecting minors and avoiding the creation of new databases that, in the wrong hands, could harm the very people they were meant to protect.
