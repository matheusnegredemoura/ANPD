apoio_EN21A: Small businesses often believe they are too insignificant to attract hackers. In reality, they are frequent targets precisely because their defences tend to be weaker. A single phishing email that tricks an employee into revealing a password can give criminals access to customer records, bank accounts and supplier details.
The good news is that basic measures go a long way. Turning on multi-factor authentication, keeping software updated and making regular offline backups would prevent or limit many common attacks. None of these steps requires a large budget, although they do require discipline.
When an incident does happen, speed matters. Firms that have a simple response plan, knowing whom to call, which systems to isolate and how to inform customers, usually recover faster and lose less trust than those that improvise.
